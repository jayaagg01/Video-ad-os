Chapter 1 8 min read

Ad Strategy & Competitor Script Spying

Uncovering high-performing ad hooks and extracting winning competitor copy for free.

Core Playbook Strategy

Never guess what script works. Use Meta Ad Library to find competitor ads running active for over 30 days. Active duration indicates profitability.

Execution Protocol

  1. Go to Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) and select your target region.
  2. Search by niche keywords (e.g. "Vitamin C Serum", "SaaS Tool", "Fitness Coaching").
  3. Filter by "Active Ads" and sort to spot ads that have been running for 30+ consecutive days.
  4. Copy the ad link and open Downsub or a transcription app to extract the spoken voiceover script.

Chapter 2 10 min read

The 16s/20s Frame Split Framework

Structuring high-converting vertical ads into 2 distinct visual frames with zero filler words.

The Word Count Limit Rule

Keep spoken text under 38 total words for a 16-second ad. Speak clearly and leave natural pauses so viewers digest the offer without feeling rushed.

Frame 1 (0s to 8s) — The Hook & Problem

Address the specific frustration or pain point. Maximum 18 words. Call out target audience immediately.

Frame 2 (8s to 16s) — Solution & Offer

Introduce product as the instant remedy and showcase the discount or call-to-action. Maximum 18 words.

Google Flow & Video Motion Generation

Converting static images into fluid 8-second video clips without visual distortion.

Preventing Motion Glitches

Use subtle camera prompt cues: "slow pan inward", "gentle eye blink", "subtle smile transition". Avoid asking for rapid movements or multi-person action to maintain 100% video realism.

Google Flow Motion Prompt Template

8-second video clip: Model holding product, smooth slow camera pan forward, subtle eye blink, gentle transition from contemplative look to a warm confident smile.

Rapid Hook A/B Testing & Scaling Matrix

Combating ad fatigue by swapping Frame 1 hooks while keeping Frame 2 solution intact.

Hook Iteration Efficiency

When an ad starts fatiguing (CTR drops), do not rebuild the whole ad. Keep Frame 2 (Solution/CTA) untouched and create 3 new Frame 1 variations to test against each other.

Angle A: Question Hook

"Tired of dealing with [Pain Point] every morning?"

Angle B: Negative Callout

"Stop making this #1 mistake if you want [Goal]!"

Angle C: Direct Callout

"Attention [Target Demographic]: Watch this before buying!"

Angle D: Secret Reveal

"The secret way to solve [Pain Point] without spending $100s."
